About Us

We are a bakery specializing in homemade desserts, custom cakes, brownies, and a wide variety of gourmet sweets. Our passion for baking allows us to create perfect sweet experiences for celebrations, special events, or simply to enjoy anytime.

Our Products

Golden Hour

A two-tier masterpiece with a lustrous gold base and ivory top, dressed in handcrafted sugar blooms. Pure romance for your big day.

Pure Bliss

Smooth, clean, and effortlessly chic. A single-tier vanilla dream adorned with a flowing cascade of fresh white orchids.

Sweet Serenity

Soft swirled buttercream meets fresh garden florals. Simple, elegant, and timeless,just like love should be.

Ivory Lace

Delicate lace embossing meets fresh white orchids and eucalyptus. A cake as beautiful as the moment itself.

Cookies & Crave

Silky milk chocolate buttercream meets a dark ganache drip, topped with crunchy Oreos and chocolate cigar rolls. Your favourite cookie, all grown up.

The Loaded

A towering chocolate buttercream drip cake piled high with Ferrero Rocher, chocolate bars and white chocolate curls. No such thing as too much chocolate here.

Midnight Indulgence

Rich dark chocolate ganache dripping down the sides, crowned with piped rosettes and glossy red cherries. For those who don't do things halfway.

Dark Romance

Velvety smooth chocolate mousse frosting dusted to perfection, topped with a hand-piped chocolate rose and striped wafer rolls. Elegant, rich, and utterly irresistible.

Cherry on Top

Fluffy pink cream dotted with chocolate chips, piled high with chocolate-dipped cherries. Sweet, cheeky, and totally extra.

Ruby Velvet

Classic red velvet coated in velvety crumbs, crowned with a cream cheese border and fresh raspberries. Bold, beautiful, and dangerously good.

Strawberry Cloud

Dreamy white buttercream with blush pink drips, gold pearl sprinkles, fresh strawberry slices and white chocolate chunks. Pretty enough to frame, better to eat.

Raspberry Rush

Rich dark sponge layers stacked with fluffy raspberry cream, dripping in a bold pink glaze and topped with a generous pile of fresh sugared raspberries.

The Classic Square

Dense, fudgy dark chocolate brownies dusted with cocoa, served alongside silky caramel sauce and fresh whipped cream. Old school never tasted this good.

Tiramisu Cups

Soft golden cupcakes piled with fluffy cream frosting, dusted with cocoa and scattered with mini chocolate chips. All the vibes of tiramisu, none of the wait.

Lattice & Blues

A golden sugar-crusted lattice pie bursting with deep blueberry filling. Crispy on the outside, jammy on the inside.

Molten Stack

Rich cocoa cookies with a gooey dark chocolate centre, stacked high and dusted with cocoa powder. Break one open and watch the magic happen.
